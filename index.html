What goes in, and what stays out

Pigments from minerals

Iron oxides and mica give colour that lasts. Every shade is tested on a range of skin tones.

Plant oils and butters

Shea, jojoba and sunflower seed oil keep lips and skin soft all day.

Nothing you'd rather avoid

No parabens, no sulphates, no mineral oil, and no animal-derived ingredients.

A five-minute routine

Four steps, one mirror, no fuss. Start with skin and finish with colour.

  1. HydrateSmooth on Aloe Cloud Moisturiser after washing your face.
  2. GlowPress two drops of Lilac Glow Serum into damp skin.
  3. FlushSweep Peach Cloud Blush along the cheekbones and up toward the temples.
  4. ColourFinish with your lip shade and a coat of mascara.
